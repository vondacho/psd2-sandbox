## 1. Problem and desired outcomes

**Problem.** The bank is an Account Servicing Payment Service Provider (ASPSP). Licensed
third-party providers must be able to reach its payment accounts on behalf of its customers:

- account information service providers (AISPs) read account data;
- payment initiation service providers (PISPs) initiate payments;
- payment instrument issuers (PIISPs) confirm the availability of funds.

The bank is building its **own ASPSP XS2A gateway** (`SRC-ADR`) and must expose that access
to Berlin Group NextGenPSD2 v1.3.16, across four systems it does not own the shape of —
Finologee (PSD2 gateway **and consent state**), Transmit (CIAM, login and SCA), PingFederate
(IDP, the token DCP accepts) and DCP (core banking).

**Desired outcomes.** None were supplied (`Q-01`). The objectives below are *derived*, and each
states how much evidence stands behind it.

| ID | Objective | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OBJ-01` | Offer a PSD2-compliant XS2A interface to TPPs for AIS and PIS | **Fact (stated constraint)** | `SRC-ADR` Bank ASPSP: "must comply with the PSD2 … regulatory requirements"; `SRC-IG` §1.1 |
| `OBJ-02` | Give PSUs control over which TPP may access what, and for how long | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` Consent lifecycle management: "strengthen customer trust"; consent screen: the PSU "grants or revokes" |
| `OBJ-03` | Authorise every TPP request with the bank's own authentication: CIAM login in the browser, then confirmation on the PSU's enrolled device | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` Bank CIAM, SCA journey, Bank Mobile App |
| `OBJ-04` | Keep XS2A operable, observable and auditable enough to prove service quality to TPPs and supervisors | **Hypothesis** | Not in the supplied sources. `SRC-ADR` names auditing as part of consent lifecycle management; targets are absent (`Q-01`) |

Two of these rows changed the design substantially against any earlier reading of the context, and
both are called out in §5: **the consent state sits at the vendor** and **the consent screen is the
TPP's**, which together leave the bank without a browser surface of its own beyond the CIAM login.

### Constraints (facts from `SRC-IG` unless marked)

- `CON-01` TLS with client authentication using a qualified website authentication certificate (QWAC) that carries the TPP's roles (§3, §4.9).
- `CON-02` A TPP may only address resources it created (§4.11).
- `CON-03` Authorisation is modelled as sub-resources of consent, payment or signing-basket resources; the ASPSP may create them implicitly and steer the TPP by hyperlinks (§4.6, §4.15).
- `CON-04` The mandatory endpoint set is fixed by §4.11; each condition is relative to its parent node.
- `CON-05` Three status vocabularies are fixed: `transactionStatus` (§14.13), `consentStatus` (§14.15), `scaStatus` (§14.16).
- `CON-06` `frequencyPerDay` ≥ 1, and ≤ 4 unless bilaterally agreed; a one-off access uses 1 (§6.3.1.1).
- `CON-07` Three consent models exist — Detailed, Global, Bank Offered. In the **Detailed** model the TPP collects the consent and **the ASPSP displays the consent details to the PSU when performing the SCA** (§6, "Consent Models").
- `CON-08` OAuth 2.0 is optional in the standard: either a PSU-authentication pre-step or the OAuth2 SCA approach (§4.3, §13).
- `CON-09` The PSU authenticates on the CIAM login screen in the browser, then confirms on an enrolled device with OTP, QR code or biometrics, and finally confirms the items of the consent on that device (`SRC-ADR`).
- `CON-10` DCP accepts only PingFederate tokens; a Transmit token must be exchanged (`SRC-ADR`).

## 3. Shared vocabulary

The same word means different things in different places. These are the collisions that matter
for this design; per-context terms live in the context map
([`../domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd`](../domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd)).

| Term | Meaning in XS2A (`SRC-IG`) | Competing meaning | Resolution proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Consent** | An AIS consent *resource* (`/v1/consents/{consentId}`) with `access`, `validUntil`, `frequencyPerDay`, `consentStatus` | The OAuth grant a PSU gives an authorisation server; the PSU's agreement to a single payment; the consent *state* Finologee manages | Say *AIS consent resource* for the resource, *OAuth grant* for the OAuth notion, *payment approval* for agreeing to a payment, and *consent record* for what Finologee masters |
| **Consent screen** | Not a term of the standard; §6 speaks of the TPP collecting consent and the ASPSP displaying its details during SCA | `SRC-ADR`: a TPP-managed browser page for granting **and revoking** | Say *TPP consent screen* for the browser page and *consent confirmation panel* for the device-side panel the ADR names |
| **Authorisation** | A sub-resource (`…/authorisations/{authorisationId}`) carrying an `scaStatus` | OAuth authorisation; permission checks in general | Say *authorisation sub-resource*; say *access decision* for permission checks |
| **Authentication** | PSU authentication — a first factor, or full SCA | TPP authentication from its eIDAS certificate | Always qualify: *PSU authentication* or *TPP identification* |
| **Token** | `Consent-ID`, an OAuth access token, or a "consent token" (§4.9 note) | A Finologee-issued TPP token; a Transmit-issued CIAM token; a PingFederate token DCP accepts | Qualify by issuer, as [`D-02`](../system/decisions/d-02-token-topology.md) does |
| **Account id** | `resourceId`, which the ASPSP may tokenise (§4.11.2, §14.20) | An IBAN; a DCP account number | Never put an IBAN or a core identifier in a path (`INV-ACC-01`) |
| **Payment** | A payment initiation resource with a `transactionStatus` | A booking in DCP | Say *payment initiation* for the XS2A resource |

## 5. Contradictions and hotspots

Each one still affects a design choice. `C-01`…`C-05` are internal to `SRC-IG`, the input this
proposal may not correct. `C-06`…`C-09` arise from the revised `SRC-ADR`, on its own or against
the specification, and they are the ones that moved this design.

| ID | Contradiction | Source | Ledger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C-01` | When a new recurring consent is authorised the former one "automatically expires" (§6.3.1.1), while §4.14.2 attaches `terminatedByTpp` to exactly that side effect | `SRC-IG` | `Q-20` |
| `C-02` | `POST /funds-confirmations` is **Mandatory** (§4.11.6), but the PIIS consent it depends on is defined outside the supplied document | `SRC-IG` | `Q-21` |
| `C-03` | Flow diagrams use status codes absent from the code lists — `ACCT`, `REJT` (§5.1.8–5.1.10), `ACTV` (§6.1.1) — against §14.13 and §14.15 | `SRC-IG` | Code lists win; `W-SPEC-01` |
| `C-04` | The consent response names the SCA-method link `selectAuthenticationMethods` (§6.3.1.1) while the catalogue and the PIS flows use `selectAuthenticationMethod` | `SRC-IG` §4.15, §14.6 | `W-SPEC-02` |
| `C-05` | `ASPSP-SCA-Approach` subsumes OAuth under `REDIRECT` (§5.3.1, §6.3.1.1), so a TPP tells the two apart only by the `scaRedirect` or `scaOAuth` link | `SRC-IG` | Informational; shapes `D-03` |
| `C-06` | The SCA journey puts the **login screen in the web browser** and then says of the journey as a whole: "The Bank Mobile App manages it. It must be presented to the PSU on the enrolled mobile device." One sentence names one owner for a journey that spans two surfaces | `SRC-ADR` | `Q-12` |
| `C-07` | The **TPP** manages the screen where the PSU "grants **or revokes**" access. Under `SRC-IG` a TPP-driven termination is `terminatedByTpp` (§6.4); `revokedByPsu` is defined as revocation "towards the ASPSP" (§14.15). No bank surface is named where the PSU can revoke towards the ASPSP | `SRC-ADR`, `SRC-IG` §14.15 | `D-09`, `Q-22` |
| `C-08` | **Finologee manages the consent state**, but `SRC-IG` holds the ASPSP answerable for `consentStatus`, for the access decision on every read, and for `ACCESS_EXCEEDED` when `frequencyPerDay` is exhausted (§6, §14.11.3). Which copy decides a read is not stated | `SRC-ADR`, `SRC-IG` | `D-01`, `RSK-07` |
| `C-09` | The device-side journey ends with the PSU confirming *the items of the consent* — which is exactly the Detailed Consent model (`CON-07`). For a **payment**, `SRC-ADR` names no panel at all, so nothing shows the amount and payee that dynamic linking requires | `SRC-ADR`, `SRC-IG` §5.1 | `Q-17` |

`C-09` is the single most consequential gap: the AIS journey has a confirmation surface that the
specification recognises, and the PIS journey does not. No artefact here invents one.
