# MVP-01 — MVP delivery pack

> **Status:** proposal generated by AI. Product owns the slice and the release commitment
> (`D-07`); compliance owns whether this is a go-live (`D-08`, `Q-44`). This pack proposes a
> coherent journey slice, not a list of high-priority tickets.

## 1. User outcome and learning hypothesis

**User outcome.** A PSU can let an account information provider see named accounts, confirm
exactly what it may see on their own enrolled device, watch that access expire or be ended by the
provider — and, separately, pay through a payment initiation provider with the bank's own
authentication.

**Learning hypothesis (`H-01`).** *PSUs abandon the authorisation at the hand-over from the
browser to the enrolled device.* The revised context makes this the central product risk: the bank
has one browser surface and everything the PSU decides happens on a second device, often a second
piece of hardware. `RM-OPS-SCA-FUNNEL` measures it, and `EVID-MVP-SCA-CONVERSION` is the evidence.

Two secondary hypotheses ride along: `H-02` (PSUs do not recognise a TPP's legal name) and `H-05`
(a PSU who withdraws access at the TPP believes they have withdrawn it at the bank).

## 3. Excluded scope, and why

| Excluded | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Consent models other than dedicated accounts | Optional in `SRC-IG` §6.3.1.2; `Q-10` unanswered. Bank-offered consent would also need a selection surface the bank does not have |
| Funds confirmation | Mandatory endpoint (§4.11.6) whose PIIS consent is defined outside the supplied document. `Q-21`, `C-02`, `RSK-04` — a go-live blocker, not an MVP one |
| Signing baskets, bulk, periodic, pain.001, instant, card accounts | Optional or `Q-23`; none is needed for the outcome in §1 |
| Multilevel SCA, corporate PSUs | `A-05`, `Q-41` |
| Payment cancellation, notification service | Optional; `Q-40`, `Q-42` |
| Embedded and decoupled SCA | `D-03`. Note that decoupled is the closer fit to the ADR's journey, and if `D-03` goes that way this exclusion is the one to revisit first |
| **A PSU-facing withdrawal at the bank** (`STORY-PSU-REVOKE`) | **`D-09` is unanswered.** There is no bank surface to build it on (`C-07`). Excluded because nobody has decided it exists, not because it is low value |
| **A payment confirmation surface** (`STORY-PIS-PAYMENT-CONFIRM`) | **`Q-17` is unanswered.** `C-09`, `RSK-08`. Excluded because inventing it would decide a compliance question on the bank's behalf |

The last two rows are the ones to read before approving this slice. Both are gaps in the supplied
context, and both may turn out to be MVP scope once the questions are answered.

## 7. Components and interfaces

LikeC4 view `mvp`. Adds to WS-01: `CMP-EVENT-OUTBOX` (conditional on `D-04`), the MVP operations
of `API-XS2A-PROFILE` and `API-PSU-CHANNEL`, `API-XS2A-EVENTS`, and `CMP-OBSERVABILITY`.
`CMP-CORE-ADAPTER` talks to a real DCP rather than a stub, which depends on `Q-05`.

## 8. Compatibility considerations

- `API-XS2A-PROFILE` is the contract TPPs integrate against: additive changes only once a TPP is onboarded, and every change diffed in the pipeline.
- `getPaymentApproval` in `API-PSU-CHANNEL` is published with `x-conditional-on: Q-17` and answers `501` until that question closes. Consumers must not treat it as available.
- `ASPSP-SCA-Approach` is `REDIRECT` (`D-03`). If `D-03` moves to decoupled, that header and the whole `scaRedirect` interaction change, so a TPP onboarding before `D-03` is settled is a compatibility liability.

## 9. Required tests

- Every generated feature in `tests/acceptance/features/` green against the running system.
- Contract tests for every `x-delivery: MVP-01` operation.
- A frequency test that exhausts `frequencyPerDay` and asserts `429 ACCESS_EXCEEDED` per account and per PSU (`INV-ACC-03`).
- A revocation test: end the consent at Finologee and assert that the next read is refused inside the agreed bound (`INV-CNS-06`, `D-01`, `RSK-07`).
- A dynamic-linking test: change the consent after the confirmation panel was shown and assert the SCA result is refused (`INV-AUT-05`).
- A resource-isolation test: a second TPP cannot reach the first one's consent or payment (`INV-CNS-04`, `INV-PAY-05`).

## 10. UX and accessibility evidence

- Usability sessions on the consent confirmation panel: does the PSU understand what they are confirming (`UX-01`, `UX-04`)? Is Decline as findable as Confirm (`UX-02`)?
- A phishing review of the CIAM login screen — the bank's only browser surface, and therefore the whole of what a PSU is taught to trust (`Q-47`, `UX-03`).
- Accessibility review of the login screen and of every app panel against the applicable standard (`Q-38`).
- Evidence on `H-05`: do PSUs who withdraw at the TPP believe they have withdrawn at the bank? It is the cheapest input `D-09` can get.

## 11. Operational measures

| ID | Evidence | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EVID-MVP-SCA-CONVERSION` | Production metric from `RM-OPS-SCA-FUNNEL` | Share of authorisations reaching `finalised`, and the drop-off between the CIAM login and the device confirmation — the test of `H-01` |
| `EVID-MVP-CONSENTS` | Production metric | Share of consents reaching `valid`; consents ended by expiry against by the TPP; **projection staleness against the agreed bound**, which is how `RSK-07` is watched |
| `EVID-MVP-OPS` | Production metric from `RM-OPS-XS2A-HEALTH` | Rate, p95 latency, error codes and availability per operation; the vendor and DCP hops separated so an incident can be routed |

No targets are proposed: none were supplied (`Q-01`). Product sets them before go-live.

## 12. Risks, dependencies and unanswered questions

**Risks:** `RSK-01` to `RSK-08`, with `RSK-07` (a vendor-mastered consent the bank answers for) and
`RSK-08` (a payment confirmed on a surface that shows nothing) as the two that could stop a
go-live.

**Dependencies:** a real DCP interface (`Q-05`), a production-shaped Finologee contract (`Q-03`),
an observability platform (`Q-06`), and CIAM configuration for all three SCA methods (`Q-14`).

**Questions that must close before MVP-01 ships:** `Q-05`, `Q-11`, `Q-13`, `Q-17`, `Q-19`, `Q-20`,
`Q-22`, `Q-23`, `Q-24`, `Q-29`, `Q-30`, `Q-31`, `Q-32`, `Q-34`, `Q-35`, `Q-38`, `Q-44`, `Q-45`,
`Q-47`, plus the decisions `D-01`, `D-02`, `D-03`, `D-08` and `D-09`.

That list is long because the supplied context is two documents. It is the honest measure of how
much of this proposal is still a proposal.
