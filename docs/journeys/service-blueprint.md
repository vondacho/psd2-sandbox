# Service blueprint — screens and read models

> **Status:** proposal generated by AI on 2026-09-19 (iteration 2). This document is derived
> from the specification and the proposal's own models. **No UX research was supplied
> (`Q-21`)**, so every screen and every piece of copy is a hypothesis for UX review (gate G1).
> The screen (`SCR-*`) and read-model (`RM-*`) identifiers are **defined here and only here**.
> Event storms, the C4 model, contracts and the manifest refer to them.

## How to read it

A blueprint separates what the PSU sees (**frontstage**: screens) from what somebody needs
in order to decide (**read models**), and from who produces that information (**backstage**:
components and contexts).

A read model is the event-storming "readmodel" card: *the information an actor or a policy
needs to take one decision*. Each read model below names:

- its consumer and the decision it supports;
- its data and the sources of that data;
- the component that owns it;
- the consistency it needs.

## 4. UX requirements proposed (hypotheses for UX review)

| ID | Proposed requirement | Why | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UX-01` | An approval screen shows **exactly** what is bound by SCA — nothing added, nothing left out | Dynamic linking (`INV-AUT-05`); detailed consent display (§6) | Security review; usability sessions |
| `UX-02` | **Decline is as easy and as visible as approve** — same screen level, no extra step | The PSU's control (`OBJ-02`) | Usability sessions |
| `UX-03` | The TPP is named as the PSU would recognise it; the legal name is always visible | `H-02`, §4.9 | Usability sessions (`Q-38`) |
| `UX-04` | Validity and frequency are expressed in plain language ("until 1 Nov 2017", "up to 4 times a day without you") | Consent fields are technical (§6.3.1.1) | Comprehension test |
| `UX-05` | Every dead end offers a way back to the TPP: expired, already used, failed, declined | TPP-Nok-Redirect-URI (§6.3.1.1); `STORY-SCA-REQUEST-UNAVAILABLE` | Funnel data: `RM-OPS-SCA-FUNNEL` |
| `UX-06` | Withdrawing access states the consequence ("⟨TPP⟩ will no longer see these accounts") and takes effect immediately at the bank | `H-03`; `RM-ACCESS-DECISION` strong consistency | Usability sessions; contract test R-REV-02 |
| `UX-07` | No bank marketing or unrelated content on approval screens | Keep the decision focused | UX review |

## 5. Accessibility

The standard is not supplied. Compliance should confirm whether the European Accessibility
Act or national rules apply, and which languages are required (`Q-42`). Proposed baseline for
every bank-managed screen above, to be confirmed:

- screen-reader labels for amounts, IBANs and dates, read in full rather than as digits;
- support for dynamic type and large text on approval screens without truncating amounts, payees or TPP names;
- contrast and target sizes per the bank's standard;
- an alternative to biometrics on `SCR-APP-AUTHENTICATE` (per Transmit capability, `Q-07`);
- no time pressure without warning: if the SCA window (`Q-29`) is short, show it and allow the PSU to go back.
