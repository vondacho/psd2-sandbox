# Service blueprint — screens and read models

> **Status:** proposal generated by AI on 2026-09-19. Iteration 2 created it; **iteration 3
> realigned it with `SRC-ADR` revision `87c72b8`:**
>
> - the bank mobile app "only serves as a channel for the last authentication factor";
> - the PSU grants or revokes a TPP's access on a **consent screen** "presented in the TPP web
>   application", whose frontend and backend come from the ASPSP gateway **or** Finologee.
>
> No UX research was supplied (`Q-21`), so every screen and every piece of copy is a hypothesis
> for UX review (gate G1). The screen (`SCR-*`) and read-model (`RM-*`) identifiers are **defined
> here and only here**. Ids withdrawn by iteration 3 are listed in §7 and must not be referenced
> by active artefacts.

## How to read it

A blueprint separates what the PSU sees (**frontstage**: screens) from what somebody needs
in order to decide (**read models**), and from who produces that information (**backstage**:
components and contexts).

A read model is the event-storming "readmodel" card: *the information an actor or a policy
needs to take one decision*. Each read model below names:

- its consumer and the decision it supports;
- its data and the sources of that data;
- the component that owns it;
- the consistency it needs.

- screen flows: [`authorisation-screen-flow.puml`](../system/uml/ux/authorisation-screen-flow.puml) and [`tpp-access-screen-flow.puml`](../system/uml/ux/tpp-access-screen-flow.puml);
- low-fi wireframes: [`wf-approve-access.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-approve-access.puml), [`wf-approve-payment.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-approve-payment.puml), [`wf-tpp-access.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-tpp-access.puml) and [`wf-app-last-factor.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-app-last-factor.puml);

## 1. Screens presented to the PSU

### Consent screen — managed by `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN`

`CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` is a web frontend with its backend. The **provider is undecided**: the
ASPSP gateway (in-house) or Finologee (`D-01`). It is "presented in the TPP web application"
(`SRC-ADR`); whether by redirect or embedding is open (`Q-44`).

## 2. Read models

### 2.1 For the PSU's decisions (frontstage)

| ID | Consumer → decision | Data (fields) | Sources | Owner (projection / query) | Consistency | Invariants / rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `RM-CONSENT-APPROVAL` | PSU on `SCR-CONSENT-ACCESS` → approve or decline a TPP's access | TPP legal name; TPP brand (`Q-38`); per account: label (`Q-37`), currency, access types; recurring or one-off; `validUntil` **as granted** (`INV-CNS-05`); `frequencyPerDay`; request deadline | `CTX-CONSENT`; `CTX-TPP-ACCESS` (names, roles); `CTX-CORE-BANKING` via `CMP-CORE-ADAPTER` (account labels, whether the PSU holds the account — `Q-35`); `CTX-TXN-AUTHORISATION` | Backend of `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN`: `CMP-PSU-CHANNEL-API` if in-house, Finologee otherwise (`D-01`) | Read at approval time, with the consent version pinned. **The approved-subject digest (`INV-AUT-05`) is computed from exactly what this model showed** | R-SCA-02, `INV-AUT-05`, `INV-CNS-05` |
| `RM-PAYMENT-APPROVAL` | PSU on `SCR-CONSENT-PAYMENT` → approve or decline the payment | Amount and currency; payee name; payee account; debtor account label; remittance; requested execution date (`Q-13`); fees (`Q-41`); TPP legal name; VoP result (`Q-16`, later) | `CTX-PAYMENT-INITIATION`; `CTX-TPP-ACCESS`; `CTX-CORE-BANKING` | As above | As above; the payment data is immutable (`INV-PAY-01`) | R-PIS-05, `INV-AUT-05`, `INV-PAY-01` |
| `RM-LAST-FACTOR-PROMPT` | PSU on `SCR-APP-LAST-FACTOR` → confirm or reject with the last factor | TPP legal name; purpose (access / payment); a short summary of what was approved — number of accounts and validity for a consent, **amount and payee for a payment** (dynamic linking, `Q-46`); request time; approved-subject digest | `CTX-TXN-AUTHORISATION` (subject, digest), via the SCA journey | `CMP-PSU-CHANNEL-API` or Transmit, depending on how the app is triggered (`Q-45`, `Q-07`) | Strong: must match the request approved on the consent screen, bound by the digest | `INV-AUT-05` |
| `RM-AUTHORISATION-OUTCOME` | PSU on `SCR-CONSENT-AWAIT-APP`, `SCR-CONSENT-OUTCOME`, `SCR-CONSENT-UNAVAILABLE` → wait, or go back to the TPP | Purpose; outcome (pending last factor, approved, declined, failed, expired, already handled); TPP legal name; return target (TPP-Redirect-URI or TPP-Nok-Redirect-URI, `INV-AUT-03`) | `CTX-TXN-AUTHORISATION` | `CMP-AUTHORISATION`, served by the consent-screen backend | Strong: reflects the decision and the last-factor result | R-SCA-03, R-SCA-04 |
| `RM-PSU-TPP-ACCESS` | PSU on `SCR-CONSENT-MANAGE` / `SCR-CONSENT-REVOKE-CONFIRM` → keep or withdraw **this TPP's** access | This PSU's consents for this TPP: TPP legal name; accounts and access types; granted on; valid until; recurring or one-off; last read (`Q-40`); status | `CTX-CONSENT`; `CTX-TPP-ACCESS`; `CTX-CORE-BANKING` (labels); `CTX-ACCOUNT-INFORMATION` (last read) | `CMP-CONSENT` (query by PSU and TPP), served by the consent-screen backend | Read-your-writes: a withdrawn access shows "withdrawn" immediately | R-REV-01, R-REV-04, `INV-CNS-04` |

*Scope change in iteration 3:* `RM-PSU-TPP-ACCESS` used to list **all** TPPs of a PSU (overview
in the app). It now covers one TPP, because the consent screen lives inside that TPP's
application. The cross-TPP overview has no channel (`STORY-PSU-ACCESS-OVERVIEW` unscheduled,
`Q-43`).

## 4. UX requirements proposed (hypotheses for UX review)

| ID | Proposed requirement | Why | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UX-01` | The consent screen shows **exactly** what is bound by SCA; the app's last-factor prompt summarises the same request (for payments: amount and payee) | Dynamic linking (`INV-AUT-05`); detailed consent display (§6) | Security review; usability sessions |
| `UX-02` | **Decline is as easy and as visible as approve**, on the consent screen *and* in the app prompt | The PSU's control (`OBJ-02`) | Usability sessions |
| `UX-03` | The TPP is named as the PSU would recognise it; the legal name is always visible; **the bank's identity is unmistakable on the consent screen** even inside the TPP's application | `H-02`, §4.9, `C-11`, `Q-47` | Usability sessions; phishing review |
| `UX-04` | Validity and frequency are expressed in plain language | Consent fields are technical (§6.3.1.1) | Comprehension test |
| `UX-05` | Every dead end offers a way back to the TPP: expired, already used, failed, declined, no app | TPP-Nok-Redirect-URI (§6.3.1.1) | Funnel data: `RM-OPS-SCA-FUNNEL` |
| `UX-06` | Withdrawing access states the consequence and takes effect immediately at the bank | `H-03`; `RM-ACCESS-DECISION` strong consistency | Usability sessions; contract test R-REV-02 |
| `UX-07` | No bank marketing or unrelated content on the consent screen or the app prompt | Keep the decision focused | UX review |
| `UX-08` | The hand-over from browser to app is explicit: the consent screen says which device will be asked, shows the deadline, and updates itself when the app confirms | Two-device SCA after iteration 3 (`H-01`) | Funnel data: approve → last factor drop-off |

## 5. Accessibility

The standard is not supplied. Compliance should confirm whether the European Accessibility
Act or national rules apply, and which languages are required (`Q-42`). Proposed baseline,
applying to the consent screen **and** the app's last-factor prompt:

- screen-reader labels for amounts, IBANs and dates;
- dynamic type without truncating amounts, payees or TPP names;
- contrast and target sizes per the bank's standard;
- an alternative to biometrics on `SCR-APP-LAST-FACTOR` (`Q-07`);
- no time pressure without warning: if the SCA window (`Q-29`) is short, show it on `SCR-CONSENT-AWAIT-APP` and in the app, and allow the PSU to go back.
