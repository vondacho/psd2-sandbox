# Service blueprint — screens and read models

> **Status:** proposal generated by AI. Iteration 2 created it; iteration 3 followed the first ADR
> revision; **iteration 4 follows `SRC-ADR` revision `7ed3eb1`**, which names three PSU-facing
> surfaces and splits them across three owners:
>
> - **Ping Federate** owns the **login screen**, in the web browser ("managed in-house by the IDP solution");
> - the **consent screen** lets the PSU "grant or revoke access to their accounts for a given TPP", also in the browser;
> - the **bank mobile app** owns the **SCA screen**, "presented to PSU on demand by the CIAM solution", where the PSU "confirms his identity and his consent as a final step".
>
> No UX research was supplied (`Q-21`), so every screen and every piece of copy is a hypothesis
> for UX review (gate G1). The screen (`SCR-*`) and read-model (`RM-*`) identifiers are **defined
> here and only here**. Ids withdrawn by iterations 3 and 4 are listed in §7 and must not be
> referenced by active artefacts.

## How to read it

A blueprint separates what the PSU sees (**frontstage**: screens) from what somebody needs
in order to decide (**read models**), and from who produces that information (**backstage**:
components and contexts).

A read model is the event-storming "readmodel" card: *the information an actor or a policy
needs to take one decision*. Each read model below names its consumer and decision, its data
and sources, its owning component, and the consistency it needs.

Diagrams: [`authorisation-screen-flow.puml`](../system/uml/ux/authorisation-screen-flow.puml),
[`tpp-access-screen-flow.puml`](../system/uml/ux/tpp-access-screen-flow.puml), and the
wireframes [`wf-approve-access.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-approve-access.puml),
[`wf-app-sca.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-app-sca.puml),
[`wf-tpp-access.puml`](../system/uml/ux/wf-tpp-access.puml). Component ownership: LikeC4 view

### Consent screen — managed by `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN`

A bank web application. Its provider is the subject of
[`D-01`](../system/decisions/d-01-consent-management.md): **Make** (ASPSP gateway, recommended)
or **Buy** (Finologee). It covers **account access** — the ADR gives it no role in payments
(`Q-46`) — plus the shared waiting and outcome pages of an authorisation (`Q-48`).

| ID | Screen | Shown when | Managed by | Reads | PSU decision / action | Slice | Stories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SCR-CONSENT-ACCESS` | "Allow ⟨TPP⟩ to see your accounts?" | PSU authenticated; purpose = consent | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-CONSENT-APPROVAL` | **Approve** or **decline** access | WS-01 (TPP name, accounts, approve / decline); MVP-01 (all details) | `STORY-SCA-REDIRECT-APP`, `STORY-SCA-CONSENT-DETAILS` |
| `SCR-CONSENT-AWAIT-APP` | "Confirm in your bank app" — with the deadline and a way to cancel | After approve (consent), or right after login (payment, `Q-48`), until the SCA screen answers | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-AUTHORISATION-OUTCOME` (polled) | Wait, or cancel | WS-01 | `STORY-SCA-REDIRECT-APP`, `STORY-PIS-SCA-REDIRECT-APP` |
| `SCR-CONSENT-OUTCOME` | "Access granted / Payment approved / Declined / Failed", with "Back to ⟨TPP⟩" | After the SCA screen answers, or after a decline | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-AUTHORISATION-OUTCOME` | Go back to the TPP | MVP-01 (WS-01 returns immediately) | `STORY-SCA-OUTCOME` |
| `SCR-CONSENT-UNAVAILABLE` | "This request is no longer available" | Redirect token unknown, expired or used; authorisation final or past its window | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-REDIRECT-SESSION`, `RM-AUTHORISATION-OUTCOME` | Return to the TPP (nok URI) | MVP-01 | `STORY-SCA-REQUEST-UNAVAILABLE` |
| `SCR-CONSENT-NO-DEVICE` | "You need an enrolled device to confirm" | The PSU has no device enrolled with the CIAM | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-SCA-CONTEXT` | Enrol, or give up — **open** (`Q-21`, `Q-49`) | unscheduled | `STORY-SCA-NO-APP` |
| `SCR-CONSENT-MANAGE` | "What ⟨TPP⟩ can see", with "Withdraw access" | PSU reaches the consent screen to manage access (`Q-43`) | `CMP-CONSENT-SCREEN` | `RM-PSU-TPP-ACCESS` | Keep, or start withdrawing | MVP-01 | `STORY-PSU-REVOKE` |

## 4. UX requirements proposed (hypotheses for UX review)

| ID | Proposed requirement | Why | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UX-01` | What is bound by SCA is shown where the PSU confirms: the consent screen for access, **and the SCA screen for a payment's amount and payee** | Dynamic linking (`INV-AUT-05`); §6 detailed consent | Security review; usability sessions |
| `UX-02` | **Decline is as easy and visible as approve**, on the consent screen *and* on the SCA screen | The PSU's control (`OBJ-02`) | Usability sessions |
| `UX-03` | The TPP is named as the PSU would recognise it; the bank's identity is unmistakable on the login, consent and SCA screens | `H-02`, §4.9, `Q-47` | Usability sessions; phishing review |
| `UX-04` | Validity and frequency in plain language | Consent fields are technical (§6.3.1.1) | Comprehension test |
| `UX-05` | Every dead end offers a way back to the TPP: expired, used, failed, declined, no enrolled device | TPP-Nok-Redirect-URI (§6.3.1.1) | `RM-OPS-SCA-FUNNEL` |
| `UX-06` | Withdrawing access states the consequence and takes effect immediately at the bank | `H-03`; `RM-ACCESS-DECISION` | Usability sessions; contract test R-REV-02 |
| `UX-07` | No bank marketing on the login, consent or SCA screens | Keep the decision focused | UX review |
| `UX-08` | The hand-over from browser to app is explicit: the waiting page says which device was asked, shows the deadline, and updates itself when the app answers | Three surfaces, often two devices (`H-01`, `RSK-06`) | Funnel: approve → confirm drop-off |

## 5. Accessibility

The standard is not supplied (`Q-42`). The baseline applies to all three bank surfaces — login
screen, consent screen and SCA screen:

- screen-reader labels for amounts, IBANs and dates;
- dynamic type without truncating amounts, payees or TPP names;
- contrast and target sizes per the bank's standard;
- an alternative to biometrics on `SCR-APP-SCA` (CIAM methods include OTP, `SRC-ADR`);
- no time pressure without warning: show the SCA window (`Q-29`) on the waiting page and in the app.
