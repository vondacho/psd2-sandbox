## 1. Problem and desired outcomes

**Problem.** The bank is an Account Servicing Payment Service Provider (ASPSP). Licensed
third-party providers need to reach its payment accounts on behalf of its customers (PSUs):

- account information service providers (AISPs) read account data;
- payment initiation service providers (PISPs) initiate payments;
- payment instrument issuers (PIISPs) confirm the availability of funds.

The bank must expose that access through an XS2A interface built to Berlin Group NextGenPSD2
v1.3.16, across systems it builds (ASPSP gateway, mobile app) and systems it runs in house
(Finologee, Ping Federate, Transmit, DCP).

**Desired outcomes.** None were supplied (`Q-20`). The objectives below are *derived*, and each
one states how much evidence stands behind it.

| ID | Objective | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OBJ-01` | Offer a PSD2-compliant XS2A interface to TPPs for AIS and PIS | **Fact (stated constraint)** | `SRC-ADR` Bank ASPSP: "must meet the regulatory requirements of PSD2"; `SRC-IG` §1.1 |
| `OBJ-02` | Give PSUs control over which TPP may access what, and for how long | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` consent lifecycle: "strengthens customer trust"; consent screen: the PSU "grants or revokes" |
| `OBJ-03` | Authorise TPP requests with the bank's own authentication: the login screen at the IDP, then the SCA screen on the enrolled device | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` Bank IDP, Bank CIAM, Bank Mobile App |
| `OBJ-04` | Keep XS2A operable and observable enough to prove service quality to TPPs and supervisors | **Hypothesis** | Not in the supplied sources. [EBA-RTS] is cited by `SRC-IG` but was not supplied (`Q-18`) |

### Constraints (facts from `SRC-IG` unless marked)

- `CON-01` TLS 1.2 or higher with client authentication, using a qualified website authentication certificate (QWAC) that carries the TPP's roles (§3).
- `CON-02` A TPP may only address resources it created (§4.11).
- `CON-03` Authorisation is modelled as sub-resources of payment, consent or signing-basket resources; an ASPSP may create them implicitly (§4.6).
- `CON-04` The mandatory endpoints are listed in §4.11; each condition is relative to its parent node.
- `CON-05` Three status vocabularies are fixed: `transactionStatus` (§14.13), `consentStatus` (§14.15), `scaStatus` (§14.16).
- `CON-06` `frequencyPerDay` ≥ 1, and ≤ 4 unless bilaterally agreed; a one-off consent uses 1 (§6.3.1.1).
- `CON-07` OAuth 2.0 is *optional* in the standard: either a PSU-authentication pre-step or the "OAuth2 SCA Approach" (§4.3, §13).
- `CON-08` The PSU authenticates on the IDP login screen, then confirms identity on the SCA screen in the bank app, on a device enrolled with the CIAM (`SRC-ADR`).

## 3. Shared vocabulary

The same word means different things in different places. These are the collisions that matter
for the design; per-context terms live in the context map
([`../domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd`](../domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd)), and the team's
own list is [`SRC-TERM`](../context/terminology.md).

| Term | Meaning in XS2A (`SRC-IG`) | Competing meaning | Resolution proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Consent** | An AIS consent *resource* (`/consents/{consentId}`) with `access`, `validUntil`, `frequencyPerDay`, `consentStatus` | The OAuth grant a PSU gives in an authorisation server; and the PSU's agreement to a payment | Say *AIS consent* for the resource, *OAuth grant* for the OAuth notion, and *payment approval* for agreeing to a payment |
| **Authorisation** | A sub-resource (`…/authorisations/{authorisationId}`) carrying an `scaStatus` | OAuth authorisation; permission checks in general | Say *authorisation sub-resource*; use *access decision* for permission checks |
| **Authentication** | PSU authentication: a first factor, or full SCA | TPP authentication from its eIDAS certificate (`SRC-TERM`, "PSD2 Authenticator") | Always qualify: *PSU authentication* or *TPP identification* |
| **Token** | The `Consent-ID` header, an OAuth access token, or a "consent token" (§4.9 note) | A Finologee-issued TPP token; a Ping-issued bank token | Qualify by issuer, as [`D-02`](../system/decisions/d-02-token-compatibility.md) does |
| **Account id** | `resourceId`, which the ASPSP may tokenise (§4.11.2, §14.20) | An IBAN, or a DCP account number | Never put an IBAN or a core identifier in a path (`Q-25`) |
| **Payment** | A payment initiation resource with a `transactionStatus` | A booking in DCP | Say *payment initiation* for the XS2A resource |

## 5. Contradictions

input this proposal may not correct.

| ID | Contradiction | Source | Ledger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C-01` | When a new recurring consent is authorised, the former one "automatically expires" (§6.3.1.1), while §4.14.2 says `terminatedByTpp` applies to exactly that side effect | `SRC-IG` | `Q-08` |
| `C-02` | `POST /funds-confirmations` is **Mandatory** (§4.11.6), but the PIIS consent it depends on is defined outside the supplied document | `SRC-IG` | `Q-14` |
| `C-03` | The flow diagrams use status codes that do not exist in the code lists — `ACCT`, `REJT` (§5.1.8–5.1.10) and `ACTV` (§6.1.1) against §14.13 and §14.15 | `SRC-IG` | Code lists win; `W-SPEC-01` |
| `C-04` | The consent response names the SCA-method link `selectAuthenticationMethods` (§6.3.1.1), while the link catalogue and the PIS flows use `selectAuthenticationMethod` and `startAuthorisationWithAuthenticationMethodSelection` | `SRC-IG` §4.15, §14.6 | `W-SPEC-02` |
| `C-05` | `ASPSP-SCA-Approach` subsumes OAuth under `REDIRECT` (§5.3.1, §6.3.1.1), so a TPP tells the two apart only by the `scaRedirect` or `scaOAuth` link | `SRC-IG` | Informational; shapes `D-03` |
| `C-06` | `SRC-ADR` names a surface for granting **account access** (the consent screen) and one for confirming **identity** (the SCA screen), but none where the PSU sees a payment's amount and payee before confirming. Dynamic linking needs one (`SRC-IG` §5.1.9) | `SRC-ADR`, `SRC-IG` | `Q-46` |
