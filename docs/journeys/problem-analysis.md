## 1. Problem and desired outcomes

**Problem.** The bank acts as an Account Servicing Payment Service Provider (ASPSP). Licensed
third-party providers (TPPs) need to reach the bank's payment accounts on behalf of the
bank's customers (PSUs):

- account information service providers (AISPs) read account data;
- payment initiation service providers (PISPs) initiate payments;
- payment instrument issuers (PIISPs) confirm the availability of funds.

The bank has to expose this access through an XS2A interface. The supplied specification is
Berlin Group NextGenPSD2 v1.3.16. Several bought products (Finologee, Ping Federate, Transmit)
and several bank-built systems (ASPSP gateway, mobile app) have to cooperate, and the
allocation of key responsibilities among them is still open (`SRC-ADR`, `SRC-Q`).

**Desired outcomes.** None were supplied (`Q-20`). The objectives below are *derived*, and
their status says how much evidence stands behind each one.

| ID | Objective | Status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OBJ-01` | Offer a PSD2-compliant XS2A interface to TPPs for AIS and PIS | **Fact (stated constraint)** | `SRC-ADR` "Bank ASPSP… must comply with PSD2"; `SRC-IG` §1.1 |
| `OBJ-02` | Give PSUs control over which TPP can access what, and for how long | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` consent section: "…enhancing customer trust", "customers have control over their data" |
| `OBJ-03` | Let PSUs authorise TPP requests with the bank's own SCA, the bank mobile app carrying the last authentication factor | **Stakeholder claim** | `SRC-ADR` Mobile App ("only serves as a channel for the last authentication factor") and SCA sections |
| `OBJ-04` | Keep XS2A operable and observable enough to prove service quality to TPPs and supervisors | **Hypothesis** | Not in supplied sources. [EBA-RTS] is cited by `SRC-IG` but not supplied (`Q-18`) |

### Constraints (facts from `SRC-IG` unless marked)

- `CON-01` TLS 1.2 or higher with client authentication using a qualified website authentication certificate (QWAC) that carries the TPP roles (§3).
- `CON-02` A TPP may only address resources it created (§4.11).
- `CON-03` Authorisation is modelled as sub-resources of payment, consent or signing-basket resources. An ASPSP may create them implicitly (§4.6).
- `CON-04` Mandatory endpoints are listed in §4.11. Their conditions are relative to the parent node.
- `CON-05` Status vocabularies are fixed: `transactionStatus` (§14.13), `consentStatus` (§14.15), `scaStatus` (§14.16).
- `CON-06` `frequencyPerDay` ≥ 1, and ≤ 4 unless bilaterally agreed; a one-off consent uses 1 (§6.3.1.1).
- `CON-07` OAuth 2.0 is *optional* in the standard. It can be a PSU-authentication pre-step or an "OAuth2 SCA Approach" (§4.3, §13).
- `CON-08` The bank builds its own ASPSP gateway and uses in-house SCA through Ping and Transmit (`SRC-ADR`).

## 3. Shared vocabulary

The same word means different things in different places. These are the collisions that
matter for the design; per-context terms live in the context map
([`docs/domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd`](../domain/xs2a-access-to-account.ddd)).

| Term | Meaning in XS2A (`SRC-IG`) | Competing meaning | Resolution proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Consent** | An AIS consent *resource* (`/consents/{consentId}`) with `access`, `validUntil`, `frequencyPerDay`, `consentStatus` | The OAuth "consent" a PSU gives in an authorisation server (Ping, Finologee) | Say *AIS consent* for the resource and *OAuth grant* for the OAuth notion |
| **Authorisation** | A sub-resource (`…/authorisations/{authorisationId}`) that carries an `scaStatus` | OAuth authorisation, or permission checks in general | Say *authorisation sub-resource*. Use *access decision* for permission checks |
| **Authentication** | PSU authentication, either a first factor or full SCA | TPP authentication via QWAC ("PSD2 Authenticator", `SRC-TERM`) | Always qualify it: *PSU authentication* or *TPP identification* |
| **Token** | `Consent-ID` header, an OAuth access token, or a "consent token" (§4.9 note) | Ping-issued internal token, Finologee-issued TPP token (`SRC-Q`) | Qualify by issuer: *TPP access token (Finologee)*, *bank token (Ping)* |
| **Account id** | `resourceId`, which may be tokenised by the ASPSP (§4.11.2 remark, §14.20) | IBAN / core-banking account number | Never place an IBAN in a path (`Q-25`) |
| **Payment** | Payment initiation resource with `transactionStatus` | Booked transaction in core banking | Say *payment initiation* for the XS2A resource |
| **eIDAS** | eIDAS (§3) | `SRC-TERM` used to spell it "eDAS" | Resolved in `44ea858`: the glossary says eIDAS |

Already decided (facts, per `SRC-ADR` revision `87c72b8`):

- Ping Federate is the bank IDP.
- Transmit is the CIAM.
- The bank builds the ASPSP gateway and the mobile app.
- SCA is in-house.
- Finologee is the PSD2 gateway provider.
- **Iteration 3:** the mobile app only carries the last SCA authentication factor. The PSU grants or revokes TPP access on a consent screen presented in the TPP web application.

Explicitly **not decided** (`SRC-ADR`, `SRC-Q`):

- Where PSU consent lifecycle management lives, and with it whether the consent screen frontend and backend come from the ASPSP gateway or Finologee.
- Whether the Finologee TPP token is exchanged for a bank token.
