## Assumptions

Each assumption is a working choice, taken so that artefacts could be drawn. Each one is
reversible and needs confirmation.

| ID | Assumption | Why it was needed | Confirm with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A-01` | The bank is an ASPSP offering payment accounts to retail PSUs under PSD2 | Frames the whole analysis | product |
| `A-02` | Finologee is TPP-facing, and the bank adapts to its contract (open-host service upstream) | Context-map power; the C4 edge | architecture, Finologee |
| `A-03` | The bank app is the PSU's SCA device, on a device enrolled with the CIAM | Every authorisation flow | iam |
| `A-04` | The target is NextGenPSD2 v1.3.16, the version supplied | All contracts | architecture |
| `A-05` | Retail PSUs first: no corporate accounts and no multilevel SCA in the MVP | Scope | product |
| `A-06` | JSON only: no pain.001, camt or MT94x | The OpenAPI profile | product |
| `A-07` | Errors use the `tppMessages` variant (§4.13.3.1) | The `Error` schema | architecture (`Q-30`) |
| `A-08` | For drawing purposes the consent state lives in the ASPSP gateway, which is the `D-01` recommendation | Sequences, C4 allocation | architecture (`D-01`) |
| `A-09` | Test data uses the spec's own example values (IBANs `DE40100100103307118608` and `DE02100100109307118603`, organizationIdentifier `PSDES-BDE-3DFD21`, ids `123cons456`, `1234-wertiq-983`, `123auth456`). | `A-11` | Combined AIS/PIS sessions (§9) are not offered in the MVP | R-CNS-07 | product |

## Withdrawn assumptions

An assumption is withdrawn when a source removes the ground under it. It is not deleted: the
artefacts that once rested on it, and the reviewers who read them, need to know it is gone.

| ID | Assumption | Withdrawn because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `A-14` | The bank app presented the approval screens of an authorisation | `SRC-ADR` (`87c72b8`) reduced the app to the last SCA factor; grant and approve moved to the consent screen |
| `A-15` | The bank app carried the PSU's overview of TPP access | Same revision: the overview lost its channel and now lives on the consent screen, one TPP at a time (`Q-43`) |
| `A-17` | A payment authorisation shows amount and payee on the SCA screen, and no payment approval page exists in the browser | `SRC-ADR` (`ca518a7`) describes the SCA screen as confirming **identity** with the final authentication method. Nothing in the sources names a surface for a payment's amount and payee, so the question is reopened as `Q-46` rather than assumed away |
